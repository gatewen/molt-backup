Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

  • Molt Backup is used only by its owner. It is not offered to any other user.
  • It only accesses files that it creates in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file scope).
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with any third party. Credentials stay on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

隱私權政策

  • Molt Backup 只由擁有者本人使用，不提供給其他使用者。
  • 只存取它自己在擁有者 Google 雲端硬碟中建立的檔案（drive.file 權限）。
  • 不蒐集、不販售、不與任何第三方分享資料；登入憑證只存在擁有者自己的電腦。
  • 可隨時在 Google 帳戶權限頁撤銷存取。
